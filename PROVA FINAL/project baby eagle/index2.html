PROTECTING MARINE LIFE

Species: Zebrasoma flavescens
Common name: Yellow Tang
Habitat: Coral reefs of the Pacific Ocean (mainly Hawaii and nearby islands)
Ecological role: Algae control and maintenance of reef health
Threats: Overharvesting for the aquarium trade, coral degradation, global warming
Fact: It has a sharp scalpel-like spine at the base of its tail used for defense, which inspires the name “surgeonfish”
Conservation: Protection of reefs, fishing regulations, and awareness about sustainable collection are essential for its preservation

"Protecting the Yellow Tang is protecting the balance of the ocean."
